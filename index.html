About

Namaste, main Prabhjot hoon

I am Prabhjot Kaur Mehra from Jalandhar. For years I was the one who showed up for everyone: my family, my friends, my neighbours. I never asked who was showing up for me.

In 2026 I started Sky Queen, after a quiet evening when my mother asked me, "Beta, tune aaj apne liye kya kiya?" I had no answer. This guide is my answer, written one honest page at a time.

  • Written in simple Hindi and English, no heavy words
  • Real stories, not lectures
  • Short chapters you can finish in one chai
  • Personal replies on WhatsApp, any time
  • Made by a Punjabi woman, for every woman and man who gives too much
